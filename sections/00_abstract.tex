\begin{abstract}
ClipAgent turns a spoken English video and a natural-language brief into a short, source-linked edit. The system retrieves timed evidence, plans clips through structured tools, checks the plan, and uses FFmpeg to render an MP4 with optional subtitles. A dialogue interface supports follow-up edits while preserving prior versions. We ran 24 executions over eight briefs with an opening-window baseline, a single-call planning baseline, and the full agent. On four held-out briefs the systems rendered 4, 2, and 4 videos, respectively. Under a five-criterion rubric the full agent passed both human checklists on one brief, with two further human disagreements. A separate AI assessment passed one full-agent brief. In a supplementary synthetic spreadsheet case, the agent preserved a visually displayed result after retrying a revision and generated a valid short clip after the user resolved a duration conflict. Cut continuity and fact coverage remain bottlenecks.
\end{abstract}
